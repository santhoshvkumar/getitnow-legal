GetItNow — Delete your account

Last updated: 16 October 2026

This page explains how to delete your account in GetItNow: Instant Loan App (GetItNow product of Kapiital Kapslock), operated by Getitnow Digital Technology Private Limited, and what happens to your data when you do.

How to request deletion

  1. Email help@getitnow.digital with the subject "Delete my GetItNow account", from the email address on your account if you have one.
  2. Include the mobile number you registered with, and your loan account number if you have taken a loan.
  3. We will confirm it is you by calling or sending an OTP to that mobile number.

You can also call our Grievance Redressal Officer, Mr. Bishwajit Ghosh, on 9875591238 (9 am to 7 pm, Monday to Saturday). We acknowledge requests within 24 hours and complete them within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, email, date of birth, address, employment and income details.
  • Your selfie, verification video and uploaded documents.
  • The references you entered, your approximate location, and your app security PIN.
  • Your login, so the account can no longer be used.

What we must keep, and for how long

  • If a loan is still open, the account cannot be deleted until it is repaid in full; the lender needs it to service the loan.
  • KYC records and loan records (identity details, PAN, Aadhaar reference, loan agreements and repayment history) are kept for 5 years after your last loan closes, as required by the RBI KYC directions and the Prevention of Money Laundering Act. They are then deleted.
  • Repayment information already reported to credit information companies stays with them under their own rules; we cannot remove it.

Deleting some data without closing your account

You can ask us to delete specific data (for example, a document you uploaded by mistake) by writing to the same address. We will delete anything we are not legally required to keep.
